Korean Folk Song Form Map

Phrase organisation and motif repetition in field recordings from the MBC Korean Folk Song Anthology (한국민요대전, recorded 1989–1996). Choose a function category, then a song type, then a recording to hear it with its phrases and repeated motifs.

Each point on the map is a median over recordings. Phrase index: higher means shorter, more even phrases (mean phrase duration and duration entropy). Return index: Motif Spread and periodic coverage. Point size: quartile of the periodicity index (texture periodicity AC and phase alignment FOLD). Grey dots are all 7,357 recordings behind the map. Highlighted recordings are the three with the most reliable automatic phrase segmentation in each third of their song type's phrase-index range.

Phrase boundaries and per-phrase lyrics are automatic (fine-tuned Whisper) and uncorrected. Motif letters come from clustering phrase pitch contours; A is the largest motif in the recording, and grey phrases do not recur.

Audio of the highlighted recordings is the MBC archive's own files from urisori.co.kr, shared for research and education with the archive's credit line. Each recording links to its page there. 자료출처: MBC 한국민요대전.
